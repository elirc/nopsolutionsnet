# 06 — Contribution Practice

Learning by shipping, at three altitudes:

1. [01-good-first-tickets.md](01-good-first-tickets.md) — 15 junior tickets (locate, follow a pattern, small diff)
2. [02-mid-level-feature-tickets.md](02-mid-level-feature-tickets.md) — 10 cross-layer tickets requiring design notes first
3. [03-senior-build-projects.md](03-senior-build-projects.md) — 6 multi-week projects with full plans
4. [04-refactor-and-design-katas.md](04-refactor-and-design-katas.md) — 8 design exercises, paper-first

Ground rules: every ticket names its blast radius and what would make a maintainer reject it; do the work locally even if you never submit upstream (if you do submit, open an issue first — see [07-career](../07-career-and-collaboration/03-maintainer-communication.md)); every completed item yields a STAR story — log it immediately in [08-interview-prep/06-behavioral-star-stories.md](../08-interview-prep/06-behavioral-star-stories.md) while the details are fresh.
